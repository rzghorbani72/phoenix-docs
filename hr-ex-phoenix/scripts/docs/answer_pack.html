Answer Pack for the HR Software Selection Criteria

The "Employee Voice" line: intelligent voice conversation and analysis of employee feedback

Proposer: MCINEXT · Audience: the Employee Experience (EX) unit and the MCI evaluation committee · Version: draft 0.1 · Date: 5 Oct 2026

This document follows the five stages and the checklist of the "HR Software Selection Guide" ("راهنمای انتخاب نرم‌افزار منابع انسانی", Chargoon, 29 Sep 2026) so the committee can compare the answers directly with its own criteria. Items in dashed yellow must be completed with data or a joint decision before the final version.

1. The problem, in process terms

Web surveys do not reach employees without a desk or computer (stores, field staff, contact center), and closed-ended answers do not reveal the "why" behind dissatisfaction. Our proposal targets three specific processes:

Process Today Expected outcome
Listening to frontline employee feedback Periodic web survey; frontline employee participation current rate: from EX; open comments are read manually. An always-on phone line with Persian conversation and follow-up questions; topic, subtopic and sentiment of every call, automatically.
Early detection of issues Issues are seen after the survey round ends, with a delay of several weeks: from EX. A trend dashboard with a baseline and an alert when a topic grows abnormally in a unit.
Answering managers' analytical questions Every new question is a report request to a data analyst. Ask in Persian and get a chart, with the query behind it shown.

Important for the next phase Read-only connection to the org structure in the HR system · scheduled outbound calls (exit interview, new-hire follow-up) · "We heard, we acted" announcements to employees Depends on MCI-IT access and an EX decision.
Future Answering HR policy and regulation questions · transactional actions (such as filing a request) No process owner has been assigned in the organization yet; not part of this contract.
What this product is not: it is not an HR record-keeping, payroll or time-and-attendance system, and it does not replace the current system. It is a listening and analysis channel that works alongside it. Employee data and all language models run inside MCI's infrastructure and do not depend on the internet or any external service.
