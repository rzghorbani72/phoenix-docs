Sensitive and emergency calls: where, when and how

Keep two paths separate. A sensitive topic (harassment, assault, discrimination, threats, misconduct) must be recorded carefully and reach a designated reviewer. An emergency situation (immediate danger to life, threat of violence, self-harm) is time-critical and cannot wait until after the call.

The core rule from the documents: missing a harassment case does serious harm, while over-flagging is more tolerable. The detection target in testing is 100% (PRD, risks section). That means detection must not be left to a single layer.

Sensitive topic, step by step

Before the pilot

Preparation

  • Taxonomy from EX
  • Approved confidentiality sentence in the KB (Q4)
  • Designate the reviewer and the response time
  • Close the IDOR and add API authentication
During the call, at detection

Two-layer detection

  • Main model, per the prompt
  • Parallel Sensitive Detector Proposal
  • If either says sensitive, it is sensitive
During the call, after detection

Behavior

  • No judgment, no interrupting
  • Do not ask for unnecessary physical or identifying details
  • Call the tool, then say the "it has been recorded" sentence
  • No promises and no predicting the outcome
Call end

Recording

  • Sensitive flag on the Kafka summary
  • No long-term memory is created Proposal
  • Alert to the reviewer
After the call

Review

  • Restricted queue separate from the dashboard
  • Human review of all sensitive cases (QA-06)
  • COA analytics as a second net
  • Two-way anonymous follow-up (ID-07, P1)

Needs that global products have solved and that we need too

Trust and anonymity

Stay anonymous but allow follow-up

Us: missing
Problem
An employee reporting harassment does not want to be identified, but the reviewer has questions in order to handle the case.
Who
AllVoices and Deel (anonymous employee reporting platforms)
How
The employee reports anonymously. HR can message them within the same case and the employee can reply, without their identity being revealed. Identity is disclosed only when the employee decides to do so.
For us
Data and channel layer: every call has one of three modes, anonymous, confidential or identified, and that mode is stored on the record. For follow-up, the employee gets a case code and a PIN and calls back with them. In anonymous mode no long-term memory is created.

ID-01, ID-04, ID-06, ID-07 · Catalog §3

Misconduct reporting with a voice assistant

Us: architecture yes, product no
Problem
The reporter speaks in their own words, but the reviewer needs an orderly case file.
Who
NAVEX (the EthicsPoint system, one of the most widely used whistleblowing hotlines), SpeakUp (Amsterdam, more than 700 customers such as IKEA and BMW), FaceUp (Czech Republic)
How
  • NAVEX Nira (1 Sep 2026): a guided two-way conversation that balances free narrative with the required case fields; multilingual; anonymity settings based on each country's law; immediate handoff to a human operator.
  • SpeakUp: the Sienna AI voice assistant takes a voice report and builds a structured case from it; voice messages in more than 75 languages; two-way anonymous chat.
  • FaceUp: a 24-hour AI line that converses, asks for context and writes the case automatically.
For us
This is the closest pattern to our IVA (follow-up questions + handoff to a human). Per the catalog red line we do not build a full whistleblowing product; only a private queue. But we should take three things: a structured case from free narrative, immediate handoff to a human in emergencies, and anonymous follow-up. No serious player in this space has been seen in Iran.

CS-09, BR-10 · Product Dossier §25.2 and §25.3

Transparency about AI and data

Us: partial
Problem
The employee must know they are talking to a machine and what happens to their data; a government buyer also wants proof.
Who
EU AI Act; HiBob (HR software)
How
  • EU AI Act: disclosing that it is AI is mandatory. Article 5 has banned emotion recognition in the workplace since February 2025. AI in hiring and employee evaluation is "high-risk" (Annex III).
  • HiBob has published an "AI trust center": customer data is not used to train models and each customer's data is kept separate.
For us
Prompt layer: the call-opening sentence that says it is AI (present in INT-01). Analytics layer: emotion only in aggregate, never on an employee's profile. Sales layer: a trust page for the buyer. Note: the current PRD decision (INT-03) has removed the recording announcement from the call opening.

SC-10, SC-11 · Product Dossier §25.8

Voluntary listening only

Us: complied
Problem
The temptation to covertly analyze employees' email and chat in order to "listen" more.
Who
Culture Amp (employee experience platform, Melbourne)
How
They consider covert message analysis "hype": only 24% of HR professionals recommend their organization's listening approach, and their own experiment showed that in analyzing work messages some groups (by region, gender, tenure) are less visible.
For us
Product red line: only a voluntary, informed channel. No monitoring of email, chat or work calls.

Sensitive cases and access

Sensitive categories and a separate permission to view

Us: missing
Problem
Comments about harassment or violence should not appear on the regular dashboard alongside everything else.
Who
Workday Peakon and Microsoft Viva Glint (employee surveys and feedback analytics)
How
  • Peakon: has a word list and a few fixed categories: violence, safety, ethics and integrity, inappropriate behavior, and custom categories defined by the organization. Two separate permissions: "read sensitive comments" and "flag sensitive comments". Next to each comment it shows which managers can see it.
  • Glint: a separate permission for managing sensitive comments. Microsoft warns that if someone has two roles, the access rights add up and a comment may be exposed.
For us
Classification layer: sensitive taxonomy from the EX unit. Detection layer: Sensitive Detector (Keyword List + LLM Classifier). Access layer: a "sensitive-case reviewer" role that sees only the sensitive queue, with every opening logged.

BR-07, PRV-10, SC-02 · Market Alignment §5a and §5c

Confidential case

Us: missing
Problem
A harassment, medical or ethics case must not reach the regular HR support teams.
Who
Workday Help and Oracle HR Case Management (the HR service desk in two large HCMs)
How
A "confidential" case type is defined. These cases are routed only to the authorized team, and others cannot even see that they exist. Routing and SLA are also configured separately for each type.
For us
Data and access layer: a separate confidential queue. Never synced to an external system. Every access is logged. Anonymous follow-up uses the "Stay anonymous" card.

CS-09, SC-03 · Catalog §8

Minimum group size in reports

Us: missing
Problem
If a manager sees the results of a three-person group, they can guess who said what.
Who
Viva Glint, Culture Amp, Workday Peakon
How
  • Glint: numbers and charts from 5 people up (can be lowered to 3); comment text from 10 people. If one group is hidden, it also hides the neighboring group so it cannot be inferred by subtraction.
  • Culture Amp: at least 5 people. A filter that takes the group below the minimum is not applied, even when combined with another filter.
  • Peakon: 5 distinct people within a year; when two groups overlap, it counts distinct individuals.
For us
The reporting API layer in coa-api. The Market Alignment document proposes: 5 distinct employees for numbers, 10 distinct employees for summaries. A phone-specific point: one person may call ten times; employees must be counted, not calls. Survey-based competitors do not have this problem.

IN-03, PRV-05 · Market Alignment §5b

Each manager sees only their own reporting line

Us: missing
Problem
The manager of one department must not see another department's data, and after an org restructuring, historical data must not shift.
Who
Qualtrics EX, Medallia, NICE CXone
How
  • Qualtrics: the manager opens the dashboard and automatically sees only their own reporting line. The org tree is updated automatically from the HR system. Historical data is shown with the structure of that time.
  • Medallia: a unit can belong to several trees at once (geographic and business).
  • NICE CXone (contact center): separate permissions for playing audio, downloading audio, viewing transcripts and exporting; redaction of sensitive information from transcripts and audio; a log for every search and playback.
For us
Data layer: OrgUnit tables (with a full path such as 1.4.17), Person with a masked code, and ScopeGrant for each user's access. On every message, store the employee's unit at the time of the call. Security layer: fix the audio IDOR and add a separate permission for audio.

IN-02, SC-02, SC-04, AN-02 · Market Alignment §5a and §6

Analytics and alerts

Rules on the call transcript, then an alert

Us: missing
Problem
A case missed during the live call must be found later, and an important issue must reach the person responsible.
Who
Amazon Connect Contact Lens (AWS call analytics); Qualtrics
How
  • Contact Lens: the manager defines rules (word or phrase, sentiment, category). If the call transcript matches a rule, the call is categorized and a Task or alert is created. Separately, it discovers new topics automatically.
  • Qualtrics: creates tickets from responses that meet a specific condition and sends them to the person responsible.
For us
The post-call analytics layer in COA: rules + LLM on the full call transcript. This is the second net for sensitive detection. If a case is found that was not flagged during the call, it goes to the sensitive queue.

AN-11, IN-05 · Catalog §9; Product Dossier §27.3c

Accurate analysis of employee feedback

Us: partial
Problem
Extract reliable topic, intent and sentiment from free text, without leaking people's names.
Who
Perceptyx (Employee Voice listening platform)
How
  • Topics via three methods at once: a model trained on labeled samples, a keyword list, and automatic discovery of new topics.
  • Intent of each comment: praise, request, suggestion, need or concern.
  • Before sending to the language model, people's names are masked with a named-entity recognition model.
  • Accuracy benchmark: four organizational psychologists labeled 1,000 comments and disagreed among themselves in 7% of cases. A small fine-tuned model reached 89.4% accuracy, almost equal to the large model but cheaper.
For us
COA analytics layer: masking names and numbers before storage, an intent column next to sentiment, discovery of new topics, and a Persian benchmark of 1,000 anonymized segments from pilot calls.

AN-02, AN-04, AN-05, AN-14 · Inspiration Playbook, Perceptyx

Automatic evaluation of every call

Us: partial
Problem
Without numbers you cannot say the assistant works correctly; listening to a few calls by hand is not enough.
Who
Google Quality AI, Observe.AI, Level AI, Calabrio (contact center quality evaluation)
How
  • Every call is scored against an evaluation form, not a sample of calls. For each answer, the evidence sentence, its timestamp in the call and a short reason are given.
  • Google has calibration sets and sampling rules for human review.
  • Form rule (Calabrio): yes/no questions, each question one observable behavior, with an explanation and an example for each option.
  • Calls the model is unsure about are flagged for human review.
For us
Evaluation layer: the same engine measures the quality of our own assistant ("Was the follow-up question appropriate?", "Did it make a promise?"). All sensitive calls get human review. The PRD scenarios are the first golden set.

AN-16, QA-01, QA-06 · Inspiration Playbook, call evaluation

Conversation quality

Targeted follow-up questions, not a form

Us: in the code, but not its rules
Problem
A vague answer like "things are bad" is not usable, but too many questions tire the employee out.
Who
Qualtrics, Perceptyx, Listen Labs (AI interviews at scale)
How
  • Qualtrics: AI reads the open answer and, if it is vague, asks one follow-up question about that same point. About 40% of respondents expanded their answer.
  • Perceptyx: conversation instead of a form; the assistant reaches out on its own at key moments (such as the end of a probation period).
  • Listen Labs: for each question a follow-up depth is set (none, only if the answer was short, once, two to three times) plus a "probing instruction" stating exactly what to look for.
For us
Prompt layer: the FUP and INF rules in the PRD. aiva layer: the clarifying branch exists; the Inspiration document proposes that each line (pilot, exit interview) have a "conversation plan" with a question depth for each topic.

BR-03 · PRD, follow-up questions; Inspiration Playbook

An assistant that does not guess

Us: partial
Problem
When a language model needs a code, date or number, it makes up something that "looks right".
Who
Moveworks (employee assistant, now part of ServiceNow)
How
  • Every required value is a defined "slot". Options are read from the system of record and the model only chooses among them. The selected value is locked.
  • It asks a question only when there is ambiguity.
  • "Resolved" is counted with separate events; if the user gives up and leaves, it is not resolved.
For us
Prompt layer: KB-01 and KB-04 (only from the knowledge base). Tool layer: for personal tasks, slot + reading from the system + verbal confirmation ("مرخصی استحقاقی، شنبه ۱۲ مهر؛ درست است؟" ("Annual leave, Saturday 12 Mehr; is that right?")). Measurement layer: if the same number calls again within 72 hours, count it as unresolved.

BR-02, BR-04, VX-08, QA-04 · Inspiration Playbook, Moveworks

A report where every sentence has evidence

Us: partial
Problem
A manager will not trust an AI summary if they cannot see where it came from.
Who
Lattice (performance management); Rippling (HR platform)
How
  • Lattice: every sentence of the report links to its specific source. If data is scarce, it writes "not enough information" instead of a generic sentence. Decisions and sending rest with a human only.
  • Rippling: the answer is shown together with its underlying query. The assistant sees only the data the user is allowed to see.
For us
Dashboard layer: each topic in the monthly report refers to a few anonymized quotes; below the threshold, it says "not enough data". AQA layer: the query and the row count next to each chart; the user's role is passed to the query.

IN-06 · Inspiration Playbook, asking the data

Closing the loop: "you said, we did"

Us: missing
Problem
If an employee does not see that what they said had an effect, they will not call a second time.
Who
Qualtrics, Viva Glint, Culture Amp
How
  • Qualtrics: only 48% of employees say they have seen the effect of their feedback; wherever the organization listened more and acted, engagement nearly doubled.
  • Glint and Culture Amp: an action plan for each topic with an owner and a due date. Culture Amp has an AI Coach for managers that helps with difficult conversations and interpreting results.
For us
Product and process layer: each main topic has an owner; an action plan with a due date and status; after each cycle, IVA says what was done in the greeting, at the end of the call or by SMS. This does not force the agent to make promises, because the organization is the one announcing the action.
